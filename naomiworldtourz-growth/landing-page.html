Important compliance

Protect trust while selling confidently

  • Never claim guaranteed visa approval, guaranteed lowest fares, or guaranteed availability.
  • Use "visa assistance" or "documentation support" unless you are legally authorized for stronger claims.
  • Show taxes, exclusions, cancellation rules, and refund timing before asking for payment.
  • Keep testimonials real, dated, and attributable.
