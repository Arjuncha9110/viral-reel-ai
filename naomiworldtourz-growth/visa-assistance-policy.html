Visa Assistance Policy

This page matters for trust. Keep the wording careful and accurate.

What assistance means

We may help with document checklists, application guidance, appointment coordination, and submission support where applicable.

What assistance does not mean

We do not promise visa approval, guaranteed processing time, or guaranteed embassy decisions.

Traveler responsibility

Travelers remain responsible for submitting accurate information, complete documents, and any required biometric or interview steps.

Fees

Separate service fees, embassy charges, courier fees, and cancellation rules should be stated clearly before payment.
