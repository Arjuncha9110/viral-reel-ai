Cancellation Policy

Use this as a draft and replace it with supplier-backed rules before publishing live.

Before booking confirmation

Proposal-stage quotes may change until final supplier confirmation is received.

After booking confirmation

Cancellation charges may apply based on hotels, airlines, activities, visa work completed, and timeline before departure.

Refund timing

Any eligible refund should be described with an expected timeline and the method of return.

Non-refundable items

Visa fees, processing charges, some flights, and certain promotional rates may be non-refundable.
