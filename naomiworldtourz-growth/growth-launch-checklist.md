# NaomiWorldTourz 48-Hour Launch Checklist

Assumptions used here:
- NaomiWorldTourz is targeting Indian leisure travelers first.
- The fastest revenue paths are family trips, honeymoon packages, spiritual trips, and premium concierge upsells.
- The brand closes best on WhatsApp or callback, not a long checkout funnel.

## 1. Publish the landing page

- Update the WhatsApp number in [`landing-page.html`](D:\Ai reel Agent\naomiworldtourz-growth\landing-page.html).
- Add the real business phone number, city, support hours, and email.
- Replace placeholder text with 2-3 real testimonials and one sample itinerary PDF link.
- Add footer links for `Terms`, `Privacy`, `Cancellation`, and `Visa Assistance Policy`.

## 2. Push three high-intent offers

- Family trip page:
  - Positioning: school holidays, senior-friendly pacing, transfers, meal clarity.
- Honeymoon page:
  - Positioning: romantic stays, photo moments, upgrades, budget ladder.
- Pilgrimage page:
  - Positioning: darshan logistics, elderly support, best months, crowd-aware planning.

## 3. Use this WhatsApp CTA language

`Send us your budget + month + destination and we'll suggest the best-fit itinerary.`

## 4. Post these three Reels/Shorts first

1. `What Rs. 75,000 gets a couple in Bali vs Thailand`
2. `3 mistakes families make when booking holiday packages`
3. `Best months for Tirupati, Varanasi, and Vaishno Devi if you want fewer crowds`

## 5. Lead handling rules

- Reply within 30 minutes during business hours.
- Ask only 4 questions on first contact:
  - destination
  - trip month
  - budget
  - traveler type
- Send one itinerary teaser fast instead of a long text wall.

## 6. Compliance guardrails

- Do not promise visa approval.
- Do not imply airline or hotel rates are locked before payment.
- Do not hide taxes, exclusions, or cancellation terms.
- If "starting from" pricing is shown, state what it includes.
