The Big Picture - 2026

Jupiter in Gemini (until Oct 2026)

Jupiter expands whatever it touches. In Gemini, it blesses communication, learning, writing, and business deals. If you have been waiting to start something - a course, a project, a conversation - the first half of 2026 is your window.

Saturn in Pisces (all year)

Saturn rewards quiet, patient effort. This is not a year for shortcuts. Spiritual practice, creative work done slowly, and long-term investments in health and relationships all carry above-average results.

Ketu Period Energy (mid-2026)

A collective Ketu period brings detachment and spiritual searching. Many people will feel drawn inward - to faith, to simplicity, to ancestral roots. Trust that pull.

Auspicious Windows in 2026

Jan - Feb: Strong for new beginnings, job changes, and starting businesses

Apr - May: Ideal for property matters and long-term financial decisions

Jun - Jul: Nirjala Ekadashi season - powerful for spiritual cleansing

Sep - Oct: Jupiter's final stretch in Gemini - excellent for learning and travel

Nov - Dec: Saturn stabilises - good for building routines and health habits
