## 3. The "Perfect" Astrology Blog Post Structure
To rank #1, every article must follow this format (Google E-A-T):
1.  **Clear Heading:** e.g., "The Spiritual Meaning of Snakes in Dreams: Vedic & Global Perspective."
2.  **Expert Intro:** "Written by [Astrologer Name] - Verified Vedic Expert."
3.  **Key Highlights:** A bulleted list of 3-5 quick takeaways.
4.  **In-depth Analysis:** Use headers (H2/H3) for different scenarios (e.g., "Snake biting," "Green snake").
5.  **Vedic Remedies:** Link back to your Choghadiya or Janam Kundli tool for "remedial timing."
6.  **FAQ Section:** 3-5 questions using Schema.org markup.
