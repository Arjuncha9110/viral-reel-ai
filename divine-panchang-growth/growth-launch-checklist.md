# Divine Panchang 48-Hour Action Checklist

Assumptions used here:
- The fastest distribution win is short-form daily astrology content, not long-form blog content alone.
- The first monetization step should be trust-building and lead capture, not high-ticket upsells.
- The brand should stay on the safe side of astrology marketing: guidance, reflection, ritual context, and spiritual education instead of fear-based certainty.

## 1. Fix discoverability before scaling content

- Verify the public site is reachable and indexable.
- Publish or confirm these pages exist:
  - `today-panchang`
  - `aaj-ka-rashifal`
  - `festival-calendar-2026`
  - `free-kundli`
  - `daily-numerology`
- Add a visible CTA on every page:
  - `Get your daily guidance on WhatsApp`
- Add footer links for `Privacy`, `Disclaimer`, `Terms`, and `Refund Policy`.

## 2. Launch one repeatable daily avatar series

- Series name:
  - `Aaj ka Divine Signal`
- Daily format:
  - 1 fast hook
  - 1 energy reading
  - 1 caution
  - 1 simple remedy
  - 1 CTA to comment the viewer's rashi
- Post to:
  - YouTube Shorts
  - Instagram Reels
  - Facebook Reels

## 3. Publish three intent-driven content buckets

- Daily utility:
  - panchang
  - rashifal
  - lucky color
  - lucky time
  - one small remedy
- Search intent:
  - kundli
  - guna milan
  - numerology
  - festival dates
  - vrat meaning
- Trust content:
  - how to read panchang
  - what astrology can and cannot do
  - simple spiritual habits without fear

## 4. Lead capture rule

- Offer one low-friction entry:
  - `Free 7-day guidance PDF on WhatsApp`
- Ask only 4 fields:
  - first name
  - birth date
  - birth time optional
  - main question: career, love, money, or peace of mind

## 5. Revenue path to test first

- Lead magnet:
  - free daily guidance or 7-day PDF
- Tripwire:
  - `₹99-₹149 personalized 7-day guidance`
- Next step:
  - `₹499-₹999 detailed report or consultation slot`

## 6. Guardrails

- Do not claim guaranteed outcomes.
- Do not exploit fear around marriage, children, health, money, or black magic.
- Do not present remedies as urgent purchases.
- If an offer is paid, show the exact price and what the buyer receives.
