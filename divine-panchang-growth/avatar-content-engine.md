# Divine Panchang Daily Avatar Engine

## Daily structure

Use this structure for every talking-avatar clip:

1. Hook
2. Energy reading
3. Caution
4. Simple remedy
5. CTA

## Hook patterns

- `Before you start your day, hear this first.`
- `If today already feels heavy, this is your signal.`
- `Your rashi may not need more effort today. It may need more calm.`
- `Here is your Divine Panchang guidance in under 30 seconds.`

## Energy lines

- `Today's energy supports clean communication and patient decisions.`
- `This is better for finishing pending work than starting emotional arguments.`
- `Momentum is available today, but only if you stay measured.`

## Caution lines

- `Avoid rushed money decisions when your emotions are already high.`
- `Do not confuse pressure with urgency today.`
- `Wait before reacting to one difficult message or conversation.`

## Remedy lines

- `Light a diya, take three deep breaths, and set one clear intention.`
- `Offer one minute of silence before your busiest task.`
- `Write one thing you release and one thing you commit to today.`

## CTA lines

- `Comment your rashi for tomorrow's reading.`
- `Save this and send it to someone who needed this signal today.`
- `Follow Divine Panchang for daily guidance that stays practical and calm.`

## Best recurring content themes

- Daily panchang
- 12-rashi rotating shorts
- Festival explainers
- Numerology day guidance
- Simple remedies with zero fear language
- Myth-vs-fact astrology education

## Monetization handoff

Soft CTA only:

- `Want a personalized 7-day guidance PDF? Link in bio or WhatsApp us.`

Avoid:

- urgent fear-based upsells
- guaranteed marriage/job/health outcomes
- manipulative black-magic framing
